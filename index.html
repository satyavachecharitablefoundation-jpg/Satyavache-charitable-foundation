About Us

Satyavache Charitable Foundation (Reg.) is a community-oriented charitable organisation based in Village Malmazara, District Hoshiarpur, Punjab. The foundation aims to contribute towards education, social welfare, youth development, health awareness, community development and other activities undertaken in the public interest.

Our Work

Education

Encouraging education and creating opportunities for children and young people to learn and grow.

Student Support

Supporting students through educational assistance, learning resources and community initiatives.

Community Development

Supporting initiatives aimed at improving public spaces, facilities and community infrastructure.

Health & Welfare

Promoting health awareness and exploring welfare initiatives for people and families in need.

Youth & Sports

Encouraging sports, fitness, discipline and positive activities among young people.

Social Service

Encouraging volunteers and community members to participate in meaningful social service.

Environment

Promoting awareness and responsible actions for cleaner and healthier surroundings.

Community Awareness

Spreading awareness about education, health, civic responsibility and social development.

Community Initiatives

  • Development and improvement of village public spaces and parks.
  • Educational kits and support for students.
  • Improvement and renovation of educational facilities.
  • Community infrastructure and local development initiatives.
  • Promotion of youth sports, fitness and healthy lifestyles.
  • Vision for a public digital library supporting students and citizens.
  • Exploring future community health and dispensary initiatives.
  • Encouraging volunteerism and participation in social service.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve society with honesty, compassion and responsibility while supporting education, health, youth development and community welfare.

Our Vision

To contribute towards educated, healthy, united and self-reliant communities where people have opportunities to live with dignity, hope and purpose.
