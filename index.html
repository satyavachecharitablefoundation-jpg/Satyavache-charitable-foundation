About Us

Satyavache Charitable Foundation (Reg.) is a community-oriented charitable organisation working for education, social welfare, youth development, health awareness, community development and the overall well-being of society.

Chairman's Message

Shri Sanjeev Goswami

"True service begins when we look beyond ourselves and work for the dignity, education and well-being of others. Every small effort made with honesty and compassion can create a meaningful change in society."


Satyavache Charitable Foundation believes that lasting development is created through education, cooperation, responsible citizenship and service to the community. Together, we can build stronger villages, brighter opportunities for children and a more caring society.

Our Work

Education

Supporting educational opportunities and encouraging children and young people to learn, grow and achieve their potential.

Student Support

Educational kits and other forms of assistance can help students continue their education with confidence.

Community Development

Working towards better public spaces, community facilities and local development initiatives.

Health & Welfare

Promoting health awareness, welfare activities and community support for people in need.

Youth & Sports

Encouraging young people to participate in sports, fitness, discipline and constructive community activities.

Social Service

Encouraging volunteers to contribute their time, skills and resources towards meaningful social causes.

Environment

Supporting awareness and activities that encourage cleaner, greener and healthier surroundings.

Community Awareness

Spreading awareness about education, health, civic responsibility and social development.

Community Initiatives

  • Development and improvement of village public spaces and parks.
  • Support for students through educational kits and assistance.
  • Improvement and renovation of educational facilities.
  • Support for community infrastructure and local development.
  • Promotion of youth sports, fitness and healthy lifestyles.
  • Vision for a public digital library to support students and citizens.
  • Exploring future community health and dispensary initiatives.
  • Encouraging volunteerism and participation in social service.

Our Mission

To serve society with honesty, compassion and responsibility while creating opportunities in education, health, youth development and community welfare.

Our Vision

To help build educated, healthy, united and self-reliant communities where every person has the opportunity to live with dignity and hope.
