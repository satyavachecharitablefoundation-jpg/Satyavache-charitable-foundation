About Us

Satyavache Charitable Foundation (Reg.) is committed to serving society with sincerity, compassion and dedication. The foundation works for the welfare and development of communities, with a special focus on education, students, children, youth and public facilities.


Through various social and community initiatives, the foundation aims to create opportunities, improve facilities and contribute towards a stronger and more developed society.

Our Work

1. Satyavache Park

Development of a public community space for the benefit and recreation of local residents.

2. School Renovation

Renovation and improvement work undertaken to improve school facilities.

3. School Construction

Construction of rooms and an additional floor of the school.

4. Children's Playground

Playground facilities developed for small children to encourage healthy recreation and activity.

5. Gym Equipment

Funds provided for gym equipment and youth fitness facilities.

6. Uniform Distribution

Uniforms distributed to students of government schools.

7. Books & Stationery

Books and stationery distributed to students to support their education.

8. School Inverter

A new inverter provided to support school facilities and uninterrupted essential services.

9. Village Development

Funds contributed towards the development and welfare of the village.

10. Digital Library

Land purchased in the village for the proposed Digital Library. Construction is starting soon.

11. Student Welfare

Support provided for education and welfare of children and students.

12. Social Service

Various other community welfare and social service initiatives undertaken according to community needs.

Community Initiatives

  • Supporting education and educational infrastructure.
  • Helping students through uniforms, books and stationery.
  • Supporting children's recreational and sports facilities.
  • Promoting youth fitness and sports activities.
  • Supporting village development initiatives.
  • Developing public facilities for the community.
  • Working towards social welfare and community empowerment.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve society with sincerity and compassion by supporting education, student welfare, community development, youth empowerment and social service.

Our Vision

To build stronger, educated and empowered communities where every individual has opportunities to grow, learn and contribute towards a better future.
