About Satyavache Charitable Foundation

Satyavache Charitable Foundation (Reg.) is a community-focused charitable organisation working towards education, social welfare, youth development and sustainable community development.

Our objective is to identify genuine community needs and work together with citizens, volunteers, supporters and well-wishers to create meaningful and lasting positive change.

We believe that education, compassion, cooperation and responsible community participation can help build stronger and self-reliant communities.

Chairman's Message

Shri Sanjeev Goswami

"True service begins when we look beyond ourselves and recognise the needs of the people around us. Every effort, however small, can become meaningful when it is guided by compassion, honesty and a genuine desire to serve society."

"Our aim should not only be to help people today, but also to create opportunities that help communities become stronger and more self-reliant tomorrow."

Our Work

Education & Student Support

Supporting students through educational materials, learning resources, student assistance and initiatives that encourage education.

Community Development

Supporting development of village facilities, public spaces, community infrastructure and projects that contribute to local improvement.

Health & Welfare

Promoting initiatives related to health, welfare, dignity and improved quality of life for people who need support.

Youth Development

Encouraging young people to participate in sports, education, social activities, volunteering and constructive community development.

Sports & Fitness

Supporting healthy lifestyles and opportunities for children and young people through sports, fitness and recreational activities.

Social Welfare

Working towards initiatives that promote social responsibility, dignity, inclusion and assistance for people in need.

Environment & Public Spaces

Encouraging cleaner surroundings, greener communities, responsible use of public spaces and environmental awareness.

Community Volunteering

Bringing volunteers and community members together to participate in meaningful social and development activities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve humanity with compassion and to create meaningful opportunities through education, social welfare, youth development and community development.

We aim to work with local communities, volunteers and supporters to identify genuine needs and develop practical solutions.

Our Vision

To build educated, healthy, responsible and self-reliant communities where every individual has access to opportunities for a better future.
