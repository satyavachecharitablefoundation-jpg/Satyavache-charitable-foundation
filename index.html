Welcome to Satyavache Charitable Foundation

Satyavache Charitable Foundation (Reg.) is committed to serving society through education, community development, social welfare and meaningful initiatives for the betterment of villages and future generations.

About Us

Satyavache Charitable Foundation (Reg.) works with a vision of creating stronger, educated and self-reliant communities. Our efforts focus on supporting students, improving educational facilities, developing public spaces, encouraging youth activities and contributing towards the overall welfare and development of the community.

Our Work

Our foundation has undertaken and supported various initiatives focused on education, village development, student welfare, youth development and community infrastructure.

Our Contributions & Completed Initiatives

Meaningful efforts made for education, community welfare and village development

✓ Satyavache Park – development of a public community space
✓ School renovation and improvement work
✓ Construction of rooms and additional floor of the school
✓ Playground facilities for small children
✓ Fund provided for gym equipment and youth fitness facilities
✓ Uniform distribution to students of government schools
✓ Books and stationery distribution to students
✓ New inverter provided for school facilities
✓ Funds contributed towards the development of the village
✓ Land purchased in the village for the proposed Digital Library; construction is starting soon
✓ Support for education and welfare of children and students
✓ Various other community welfare and social service initiatives

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve society with sincerity and compassion by supporting education, student welfare, community development, youth activities and initiatives that improve the quality of life of people.

Our Vision

To build stronger, educated, healthy and self-reliant communities where every child receives opportunities to learn, grow and contribute positively to society.
